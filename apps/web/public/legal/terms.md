## 1. What Berth actually does

You submit an idea. A coin for that idea is launched on pump.fun. The creator fees that coin earns are collected by the platform and spent on an AI agent that writes, deploys, and maintains the app. Money the app earns is used by the platform to buy the app's coin on the open market and burn it.

Berth therefore does three things: it runs the build agent, it hosts the apps, and it processes payments made inside the apps.
